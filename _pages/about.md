Hey there 👋! You can call me Gilberto. I'm currently a PhD. student in the Computer Science departament at <a href="https://icmc.usp.br/">ICMC</a> in the <a href="https://www5.usp.br/">University of Sao Paulo</a> with emphasis on Artificial Intelligence and Quantification. I'm working as a researcher at LABIC under the supervision of prof. <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=HXu5lB4AAAAJ&hl=pt-BR&oi=ao">Diego Furtado Silva</a> with Quantification for Complex Scenarious. I also earned my MSc. degree at the University of São Paulo under the supervision of Prof. Diego Furtado, where I applied deep learning techniques for time series to classification and one-class learning tasks.

I've made my BSc. degree at <a href="https://ifsc.edu.br/">Federal Institute of Santa Catarina</a> under the supervision of prof. <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Cp3hDBMAAAAJ&hl=pt-BR&oi=ao">Carlos Andres Ferrero</a>, there I worked with an algorithm to transform brain tumor images into time series structures and then applying an unsupervised clustering algorithm to group the structures into clusters and after that analysing each tumor clusters.

Some of the machine learning applications I am interested in include biodiversity, bioestimation, public policy analysis, and applications in health and biology.
